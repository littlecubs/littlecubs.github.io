LittleCubs Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

LittleCubs is a stories and learning app for children aged 3 to 10, used together with a parent or carer. It is published by Tamil Arul. M, India ("we", "us"). You can contact us at littlecubsapp@gmail.com.

In short

  • We do not collect your or your child's personal information. There is no account, no sign-up and no email address to give.
  • Everything you set up in the app, such as child profiles, favourite stories, saved words, stars, badges, progress and drawings, is saved only on your phone. We never receive it.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and we never sell data.
  • The app's own code does not send or fetch anything over the internet. All stories and sounds are inside the app. The only internet contact is Google's own Google Play billing component, for subscriptions.
  • The Parents Area is protected by a PIN. Premium is bought through Google Play, so we never see your card or bank details.

1. Children and parents

LittleCubs is designed for children, so it collects as little as possible: nothing is sent to us. A child never needs to type a name, an email address or any other personal detail. A parent can add a name or nickname, an age, an avatar and a language for a child in the Parents Area, but these are used only to personalise the app on that phone and stay on that phone.

2. What the app saves on your phone

Saved on your phoneWhy
Child profiles: name or nickname, age, avatar, languageTo greet the child and choose suitable stories and games.
Favourite stories and saved words (My Words, Word Garden)To show what a child has saved.
Stars, badges, reading statistics, time spent, game progress, activity historyTo show progress and the weekly summary for parents.
Screen-time rules, bedtime mode and other settings a parent choosesTo apply the limits a parent sets.
Drawings, Gallery pictures and My World buildsChildren's own creations.
The parent PIN (stored encrypted)To keep the Parents Area for grown-ups.

This information never leaves your phone through our app. If you uninstall LittleCubs, or clear its storage in your phone's settings, it is removed.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not run any servers that store information about you or your child.
  • We do not show advertisements, and the app contains no advertising tools.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking to follow children's behaviour.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade information.
  • We do not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • We do not let children contact other people or share anything from inside the app.
  • We do not look up words or stories online. Word meanings come from a list inside the app.
  • The app may check whether your phone has a connection, only to show a friendly note. It sends nothing when it does.

4. Subscriptions and Google Play

LittleCubs Premium is a paid subscription. It is bought, billed and managed by Google Play. Google processes your payment details under Google's Privacy Policy. The app uses Google's Play Billing component to ask Google Play, through the Play Store app on your phone, only whether your Google account has an active subscription, so it can unlock Premium. That component is made by Google and may exchange technical information with Google to do its job, under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see your card or bank details, name or email. You can cancel at any time in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

5. Links that open outside the app

Some links, such as this privacy policy or your subscription page in Google Play, open in your phone's browser or in the Play Store. Those sites have their own privacy policies. The app itself does not send any information when you open them. These links are inside the Parents Area.

6. Your phone's own backup

LittleCubs asks Android not to include its data in phone backups, so what the app saves stays on that phone only. A new phone starts with a fresh app.

7. Your choices

  • Remove a child's profile: Parents Area, then My Family.
  • Remove everything saved by the app: uninstall LittleCubs, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then LittleCubs, then Storage, then Clear data.
  • Cancel Premium: in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

Because we hold no personal information about you or your child, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a concern, write to littlecubsapp@gmail.com. We reply within 10 working days.

8. If this changes

If a future version of LittleCubs collects any information, we will ask a parent for permission first inside the app, and update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
